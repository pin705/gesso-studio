# Sugar Rush - Art Bible

## Direction
Match-3 puzzle game for mobile portrait. Bright, sweet, bouncy: candy-glossy UI over a pastel sky. Built from the casual style pack.

## Targets
1080×1920 portrait, authored at 1x (540×960 reference), exported @2x and @3x. Buttons 380×150, popups 360×300, gems 128×128.

## Light
Soft top light. Every shape gets a glossy top highlight and a darker lip below; shadows are violet (#241640), never gray.

## Palette
- Outline and shadow: #241640 #3a2a66
- Grass (primary action): #1d5410 #2e8519 #4fb526 #86dd45 #c9f98e
- Sun (rewards, progress): #7a4100 #c77d00 #ffbf14 #ffe060 #fff5bf
- Sky (panels, boosters): #0d3a7a #1764cc #3894ff #86c6ff #d8efff
- Candy (titles, ribbons): #6e0f3f #b81d6a #f74a9d #ff9ccd #ffd6ea
- Cream (popup body): #8a6a9e #c9b8e0 #efe8fb #fbf8ff #ffffff
The gems use candy, sky, grass and sun as their base colors.

## Materials
Glossy candy plastic, cream enamel, faceted gems rendered in 3D.

## Shape language
Round, chunky, soft: pills, bubbles, big corner radii. Thick 4px violet outlines.

## UI kit rules
Outline 4px #241640; lip 10-12px in the darker ramp step; gloss band on the top 40%. Labels in Lilita One with a dark outline; body text in Baloo 2.

## Icons
Chunky silhouettes with candy material and gloss; empty states in #3a2a66.

## VFX
Pink and white bursts with sparkles; short and bouncy.

## Don'ts
Thin lines, dark or gritty textures, realistic metal, gray shadows.
