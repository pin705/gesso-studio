# Ashen Crown - Art Bible

## Direction
Grim action RPG for PC. A fallen kingdom of soot, forged iron and old gold. Heavy, worn, sacred-and-profane; every surface has been used, burnt or bled on. Built from the dark-fantasy style pack, with gold kept rare and meaningful.

## Targets
1920×1080 landscape, authored at 1x, exported @1x and @2x. HUD orbs 200×200, item slots 96×96, skill icons 128×128, menu buttons 320×96.

## Light
One warm torch key from the top-left; 60-70% of each form in shadow. Shadows lean cool blue-violet (#1b1f24 family, never black), a faint cool rim (#7b848c) on the shadow side separates forms from the dark HUD.

## Palette

- Old gold (legendary, focus): #3a2410 #6b4518 #a8742a #d8a84e #f2d692
- Bone and parchment: #2b241a #4d4231 #7a6c52 #aa9b7b #d6cbb0
- Blood (health, CTA): #1f0405 #4a0b0d #7d1414 #b0221c #d94a2b
- Ember (emissive): #e8561e #ff9a3c #ffd9a0
- Arcane (mana, magic): #120c24 #251a4a #3f2d7a #6a52c4 #a894f0 #dcd2ff
- Rarity: common iron #9aa3aa, rare arcane #6a52c4, legendary old gold #d8a84e
60/30/10: iron dominates, bone and leather support, blood, arcane and gold are accents.

## Materials
Blackened hammered iron with chipped bright edges and rivets; old gold with worn highlights (never polished chrome-bright); bone and parchment; glowing liquids in glass orbs.

## Shape language and ornament
Heavy, angular, asymmetric. Gothic points, spikes, thorny ironwork, rivets, cracked edges. Ornament clusters at the top and corners; the bottom stays heavy and plain.

## Line and edges
Outline #0b0d10, 2-3px, chipped rather than smooth. Worn edges get 1px light scratches (#9aa3aa at 60%).

## UI kit rules
Chamfered corners of 6px; bevel 3px; drop shadow 0 6 10 #0b0d10 at 60%. Buttons are iron plates with a blood-red enamel face for the primary action. Labels #d6cbb0 with a #0b0d10 outline. Rarity is shown by the slot border, never by recoloring the item.

## Icons
Skill icons are full-bleed square paintings with a strong central motif, lit by the skill's own light. Item icons are the object alone, 3/4 view, 2-3px outline.

## VFX
Embers, smoke, blood and dark arcane: hot white-yellow core, saturated hue, dirty dark edges.

## Don'ts
Clean shiny metal, cute rounded shapes, saturated rainbow colors, pure black, gore as decoration, glow on anything that does not burn or cast magic.
