# Azure Cloud Sect - Art Bible

## Direction
Xianxia cultivation RPG for PC and mobile. Serene and precious: jade, old gold and red lacquer over misty mountains and silk. Built from the xianxia style pack.

## Targets
1920×1080, authored at 1x (960×540 reference), exported @1x and @2x. Buttons 380×132, panels 360×300, slots 110×110, icons 96×96.

## Light
Soft daylight from the top-left through mist; jade glows faintly from within; gold carries the brightest highlights.

## Palette
- Ink outline: #0e1113
- Jade: #0c2e30 #154b45 #20705f #3a977d #78c7a5 #d3f0dc
- Old gold: #3a1e08 #6b3b10 #a86a1f #d9a441 #f3d27c #fff4d2
- Red lacquer: #3b0d0d #6e1a17 #a52b22 #d0493a #ee8a6b
- Silk: #8f7a55 #c2ae85 #e0d3b4 #f3ecdb #fffaf0
60/30/10: silk and mist dominate, jade structure, gold and lacquer accents.

## Materials
Carved jade with clouded veins, worn old gold, glossy red lacquer, silk paper.

## Shape language
Flowing curves: xiangyun cloud scrolls, lotus, round medallions, symmetric but soft.

## UI kit rules
Gold rims 6px around jade or lacquer faces; cloud-scroll caps on buttons; round medallion slots. Titles in Ma Shan Zheng, body in Cinzel.

## Icons
Treasures as silhouettes in their material: elixirs in lacquer, seals in jade, spirit weapons in gold with a soft inner glow.

## VFX
Qi: white-gold core, jade body, deep teal edge; slow, breathing loops.

## Don'ts
Iron, rivets, spikes, neon colors, grunge.
