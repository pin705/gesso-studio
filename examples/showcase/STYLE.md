# Showcase - Art Bible

This project deliberately mixes genres to show that nothing in the studio is tied to one style. Each asset names its style pack in `data-style` and follows that pack from `knowledge/styles/`. A real game has exactly one art bible, and every asset obeys it.

## Direction
One asset per genre, each made with the standard workflow (block-in, form, polish, critique loop):
- `casual-btn-play` and `casual-btn-play.pressed`: casual mobile, candy gloss with a thick lip.
- `fantasy-potion-health`: dark fantasy, backlit glass and forged iron.
- `xianxia-btn-jade`: xianxia, carved jade with gold xiangyun medallions, 9-slice.
- `scifi-panel-holo`: sci-fi HUD, chamfered holographic glass, 9-slice.
- `vfx-slash-arc`: heroic-fantasy energy slash, 0.5s flipbook.

## Shared rules
Light from the top-left at about 45°. Transparent canvases, textless UI chrome, ids prefixed with the asset id, authored at 1x.
