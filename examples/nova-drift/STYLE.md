# Nova Drift - Art Bible

## Direction
Arcade space shooter for PC and console. A pilot's cockpit HUD over deep space: clean, precise, holographic. Built from the sci-fi style pack.

## Targets
1920×1080, authored at 1x, exported @1x and @2x. Buttons 320×96, panels 360×260, ability slots 104×104 with 64×64 glyph icons.

## Light
UI is emissive, not lit: holograms glow from within and scan lines run horizontally. Space is dark; the only warm light is amber warnings.

## Palette
- Hull and void: #04070c #0a1019
- Holo cyan: #108aa6 #22c3e6 #7fe8ff #dcfaff
- Warning amber: #d98a00 #ffb52e #ffe0a0
60/30/10: void dominates, cyan structure, amber only for warnings and alerts.

## Materials
Hologram plates with scan lines and a 1px bright edge; corner brackets; segmented bars.

## Shape language
Chamfered 45° corners, brackets and ticks, thin lines. No rounded pills, no ornament.

## UI kit rules
Chamfer 10-24px; 1px inner line at 60% cyan; corner brackets 2.5px. Labels in Orbitron (titles) and Rajdhani (body). Pressed states brighten the plate.

## Icons
Ability glyphs are emissive silhouettes in the holo ramp with a soft cyan glow, 64×64.

## VFX
Plasma: white-cyan core, cyan body, deep teal edge. Fast and short.

## Don'ts
Wood, gold, parchment or any painted texture; warm colors except amber warnings; glow on the void.
