# Style pack: Xianxia (Eastern cultivation fantasy)

**Mood**: serene, precious, misty, ascendant. Carved jade and gold filigree floating in ink-wash mist; elegance over bulk.

## Light
Soft diffuse key from the top-left, as if through clouds. Shadows are cool teal-gray, never black; gold shadows stay warm brown. Emissive qi adds a pale cyan-green rim to nearby surfaces.

## Palette (ramps, dark to light)
- Jade: #0c2e30 #154b45 #20705f #3a977d #78c7a5 #d3f0dc
- Imperial gold: #3a1e08 #6b3b10 #a86a1f #d9a441 #f3d27c #fff4d2
- Cinnabar lacquer (accent, sparingly): #3b0d0d #6e1a17 #a52b22 #d0493a #ee8a6b
- Ink neutrals: #0e1113 #1b2226 #2e3a3d #4e5d5d #7d8b86
- Silk and paper: #8f7a55 #c2ae85 #e0d3b4 #f3ecdb
- Qi (emissive only): #1f9e86 #4fd6b3 #a8f5dc #effff9
60/30/10: jade or ink dominant, gold supporting, cinnabar and qi as accents.

## Shape language and ornament
Flowing S-curves and circles (moon gates, discs), octagons (bagua) and tall plaques; formal symmetric frames softened by organic ornament. Motifs: xiangyun cloud scrolls, ruyi heads, lotus petals, simplified dragon or phoenix silhouettes, mountain ridges, tassels and knots. Ornaments hug corners and joints; cloud scrolls curl inward toward the content.

## Line and edges
Calligraphic lines: thick-thin, tapered ends, drawn as filled paths. Jade has almost no outline (edges defined by value and a bright rim); gold uses thin dark separations. Ink outlines, where used, are #0e1113 at 70-90% with a subtle wobble.

## Materials
Jade (translucent, milky highlights, inner glow opposite the light), gold filigree (thin, bright speculars), red lacquer (glossy, deep), silk and rice paper (matte, fiber grain), aged bronze (green patina in crevices).

## UI kit
Jade plaques with gold trim and cloud-scroll corners for buttons; lacquer or silk panels framed in gold with tassel hangers; jade tube bars with gold end caps; round medallion icon frames. Corner radius family 6/12/24, bevel 3-4px, drop shadow 0 6 10 #0c2e30 at 40%. Labels: #f3ecdb with a #0c2e30 outline.

## VFX
Qi wisps as tapered ribbons, sword light as a thin white-cyan crescent, ink bleeding and diffusing, drifting petals or talisman paper. Motion flows: slow-in slow-out, long graceful trails.

## Don'ts
Neon greens; red-and-gold everywhere; random ink splatter as decoration; thick black outlines; Japanese motifs (torii, sakura) unless the game mixes them intentionally; heavy grime.
