# Style pack: Sci-fi (holographic HUD and hard surface)

**Mood**: precise, cold, advanced, informative. Thin emissive lines on deep space glass; engineered, modular, purposeful.

## Light
Mostly self-lit: emissive edges and data, dark environment. Hard-surface parts get a cool overhead key with sharp specular edges. Glows are thin and restrained.

## Palette (ramps, dark to light)
- Deep space: #04070c #0a1019 #101a28 #182638 #22354c
- Holo cyan (primary): #062c38 #0b5468 #108aa6 #22c3e6 #7fe8ff #dcfaff
- Amber (warning, secondary action): #332000 #7a4a00 #d98a00 #ffb52e #ffe0a0
- Danger: #3d0710 #99132b #e6334d #ff8595
- Steel: #1f252d #343d48 #525d6b #7b8796 #b3bcc7
- Cyberpunk variant: night #0b0616 #1a0f2e #2d1a4d, magenta #3d0a36 #8f1680 #e02ac8 #ff7ae6, acid #6b6400 #d9cd1a #fff36b
60/30/10: space dominant, cyan supporting, amber and danger as accents.

## Shape language and ornament
Chamfered 45° corners (often only two opposite corners), straight runs, modular segments, brackets, tick marks, grids, hexagons and triangles, header tabs. Asymmetry is intentional and engineered.

## Line and edges
Line hierarchy at 1x: 1px detail, 2px structure, 3px focus. Pixel-snapped (odd widths on .5 coordinates). Double lines and dashed segments add rhythm. No wobble.

## Materials
Holographic glass (dark fill at 60-85% opacity, bright edges, faint scanlines), brushed metal, carbon fiber, emissive strips.

## UI kit
Chamfered panels with corner brackets and a header tab; segmented bars; hex icon frames; buttons as chamfered plates with an emissive rim, pressed state fills with cyan at 25%. 9-slice insets at the chamfers. Labels: #dcfaff, no outline, uppercase tracking by the engine.

## VFX
Energy beams and pulses (white core, cyan, deep blue edge), shield hex ripples, scan sweeps, glitch displacement, plasma bursts. Fast, precise, linear-to-ease-out timing.

## Don'ts
Glow on every line; random circuit-board filler; unreadable micro text; too many hues at once; soft blobby shapes.
