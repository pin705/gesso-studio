# Style pack: Dark fantasy (grim, gothic, action RPG)

**Mood**: heavy, dangerous, worn, sacred-and-profane. Forged iron, cracked bone, old leather and a single ember of warmth.

## Light
One dramatic warm key from the top-left, about 60-70% of each form in shadow. Deep shadows lean cool blue-violet; a faint cool rim on the shadow side separates forms from dark backgrounds. Embers and magic are the only saturated light.

## Palette (ramps, dark to light)
- Iron: #0b0d10 #171b20 #262c33 #3c444d #5f6973 #9aa3aa
- Bone and parchment: #2b241a #4d4231 #7a6c52 #aa9b7b #d6cbb0
- Leather: #1c120b #342116 #533522 #7a5233
- Blood (CTA, danger, health): #1f0405 #4a0b0d #7d1414 #b0221c #d94a2b
- Ember (emissive): #e8561e #ff9a3c #ffd9a0
- Arcane (rare accent): #1e1230 #3b2463 #6a45a6 #a888e6
60/30/10: iron and leather dominate, bone supports, blood and ember accent.

## Shape language and ornament
Angular, heavy, asymmetric; pointed gothic arches, spikes, thorns, chains, rivets, bands of hammered metal; skulls and sigils sparingly. Silhouettes are top-heavy and aggressive.

## Line and edges
Dark, weighty outlines (#0b0d10, 2-3px at 1x) with chipped irregularity. Edges are worn: lighter scratched highlights on exposed corners, grime in recesses.

## Materials
Forged or blackened iron (dents, hammer marks, edge wear), worn leather with stitching, bone and parchment, rust, cracked stone, dried blood, glowing embers.

## UI kit
Heavy iron frames with corner rivets and spikes over leather or dark stone; parchment content wells; blood-red primary buttons used sparingly; bars as iron housings with deep recessed tracks. Low overall saturation; value contrast carries the hierarchy. Labels: #d6cbb0 with #0b0d10 outline.

## VFX
Embers and sparks, heavy smoke, blood spray, dark magic (violet-black with a hot bright core), dirty fire with black smoke. Snappy impacts, slow lingering smoke.

## Don'ts
Clean shiny surfaces; saturated rainbow palettes; cute rounded shapes; gore as decoration; ornament so busy that the silhouette is lost.
