# Style pack: Heroic fantasy (bright, chunky, hand-painted look)

**Mood**: adventurous, warm, inviting, readable at a glance. Big gems, thick gold trims, carved wood and sunlit stone, exaggerated proportions.

## Light
Warm sunlight from the top-left, strong highlights, colored bounce light (warm from below on gold, cool sky light on tops of stone). Soft painted gradients for form, crisp specular pops on metal and gems.

## Palette (ramps, dark to light)
- Gold: #4a2806 #7d4a0f #b87a1c #eab13a #ffdf86 #fff6d8
- Wood: #2e1a0d #4d2f18 #764a27 #a26c3c #c99563
- Stone: #262833 #3d404d #5d616f #858a98 #b2b6c2
- Royal blue: #0c1a45 #172f7a #2553b8 #4f86e8 #9cc2ff #dcebff
- Leaf green (CTA): #12330f #24591d #3f8a2e #6cbf45 #b2e67f
- Crimson: #3d0a10 #721822 #ad2a36 #e04f55 #ff9a92
- Gems: amethyst #3d1566 #7a3cc2 #c28cff, emerald #0a4a33 #139866 #6ff0b1, ruby #5c0617 #b8142f #ff6a7c
- Outline: #24130a
60/30/10: wood or stone dominant, gold supporting, gems and CTA green as accents.

## Shape language and ornament
Chunky, sturdy, rounded-rectangular with bold bevels (4-8px at 1x); exaggerated scale (oversized gems, thick frames); heraldic shields, banners and ribbons, rivets, filigree scrolls, crowns.

## Line and edges
Dark warm outline #24130a, 2-3px, a little heavier on the bottom-right. Soft form edges, crisp edges on metal and gem facets.

## Materials
Polished gold, carved and planked wood, cut gems with internal glow, sunlit stone, leather straps, cloth banners.

## UI kit
Wooden or stone panels with gold trim and gem corners; ribbon header banners; big rounded green or gold primary buttons with a thick lip; bars in gold housings. Radius family 8/16/28; drop shadow 0 6 8 #24130a at 45%. Labels: #fff6d8 with #24130a outline.

## VFX
Sparkles and star glints, bright magic swirls, colored impact rings, gold coins bursting. Punchy, bouncy timing.

## Don'ts
Muddy desaturation; thin wispy lines; realistic grime; tiny unreadable details; flat single-gradient shading.
