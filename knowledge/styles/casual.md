# Style pack: Casual mobile (candy, puzzle, party)

**Mood**: joyful, tactile, bouncy, instantly readable. Glossy candy shapes with thick lips you want to press.

## Light
Bright top light, big glossy highlights, soft drop shadows. Each object is one hue ramp; shading moves along it with a warm highlight and a cooler, more saturated shadow.

## Palette (ramps, dark to light)
- Grass green (primary CTA): #1d5410 #2e8519 #4fb526 #86dd45 #c9f98e
- Sky blue: #0d3a7a #1764cc #3894ff #86c6ff #d8efff
- Sun yellow: #7a4100 #c77d00 #ffbf14 #ffe060 #fff5bf
- Candy pink: #6e0f3f #b81d6a #f74a9d #ff9ccd
- Orange: #7d2e00 #d95a07 #ff9124 #ffc27a
- Grape: #34106a #5f25b8 #9259ff #c7a6ff
- Outline: #241640; gloss: #ffffff
60/30/10: a light background family, bright element hues supporting, the CTA green as the accent.

## Shape language
Round and bubbly: corner radius at least 30% of the height, pill buttons, circles, soft stars and hearts. Chunky proportions, big and simple; shapes designed to squash and stretch.

## Line and edges
Thick dark outline #241640 (3-6px at 1x), slightly heavier at the bottom. Clean smooth edges, no texture.

## Construction (the classic chunky button)
Soft drop shadow, then the outline silhouette, then the lip (darkest-but-one step, 6-10px below the face), then the face (vertical gradient from the light step to the base step), a gloss capsule over the top 35-45% (white 55% fading to 0%), and one or two small specular dots at the top-left.

## UI kit
Pill buttons, round icon buttons, popups with thick borders and ribbon headers, star ratings, currency pills with an icon well on the left. Radius family 16/28/999. Labels: #ffffff with #241640 outline.

## VFX
Stars, confetti, sparkle bursts, coin trails; overshoot pops (scale 0 to 1.15 to 0.95 to 1).

## Don'ts
Thin lines; dark or moody palettes; realistic textures; pure black outlines; more than one hue per object.
