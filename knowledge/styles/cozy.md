# Style pack: Cozy (farming, life sim, storybook)

**Mood**: warm, handmade, gentle, safe. Painted wood signs, paper notes and leaves, like a village noticeboard on a sunny afternoon.

## Light
Warm afternoon sun from the top-left. Soft shadows lean brown-violet, never gray; highlights are warm cream. Low contrast overall, with the strongest contrast kept for the call to action.

## Palette (ramps, dark to light)
- Wood: #3a2414 #5c3a1e #8a5a2e #b98347 #dcae6e
- Leaf green (primary action): #1f3d1c #2f5e27 #4a8a36 #74b44f #a8d77a #d9f0b0
- Paper: #6b5a45 #a8916f #d8c39c #efe1c2 #fbf4e3
- Berry (alerts, badges): #5c1620 #9b2e30 #d0543f #f08a5d #f7b98f
- Sun gold (rewards): #6e4310 #b5791f #e8b341 #fbe08a #fff6d0
- Outline: #3a2414
60/30/10: paper and sky dominate, wood frames support, leaf green and berry are the accents.

## Shape language
Soft rectangles with generous radii, slightly irregular like cut wood; leaves, sprouts, nails and stitches as ornament. Ornament grows from the structure (a leaf tucked behind a sign, nails at the corners).

## Line and edges
Warm dark-brown outline #3a2414, 2.5-3px, soft rather than crisp. Painted edges get a thin lighter inner line.

## Construction (the painted sign button)
Drop shadow, outline, a wooden lip below (wood ramp), the painted face (leaf green over visible wood grain), a thin light inner border, one ornament at the top-left corner (leaves with a berry) and a nail on the far side.

## UI kit
Wood-framed noticeboard panels with a paper well and corner nails; wooden bars with a sun badge; crate slots. Labels in Fredoka or Baloo 2, cream #fbf4e3 with a brown outline.

## Icons
Crops, tools and produce as chunky silhouettes in their own ramps, two colors at most (a carrot's orange body and green leaves), soft rim light.

## VFX
Sparkles, leaves and dust motes drifting slowly; warm gold glints on harvest. Gentle, never explosive.

## Don'ts
Neon, pure black, hard metal, grunge, skulls, harsh contrast, fast aggressive motion.
